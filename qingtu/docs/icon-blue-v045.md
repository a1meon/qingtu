# 轻图蓝色应用图标

使用内置 image_gen 生成。原始透明 PNG：build/source/app-icon-blue.png。
工程图标：build/icon.png、build/icon.icns、build/icon.ico；浏览器及标题栏：public/assets/app-icon.png。
执行 npm run icons 可从同一画稿重新生成各平台尺寸，仅做缩放和格式包装。

生成提示词：
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final macOS and Windows app icon for Qingtu, a friendly blue image compression/resizing app. Create one square app icon, straight-on orthographic front view, centered large rounded squircle tile occupying 88% of canvas, genuine transparent background outside its silhouette. Style: polished playful tactile 2.5D illustration with bold simple shapes, gentle embossed depth and a chunky darker blue bottom rim like a friendly raised UI button. Saturated sky blue #1cb0f6 tile, subtle cyan upper light, dark blue #0897db bottom edge. On tile: a large warm white image-document symbol with a softly folded upper-right corner, two small navy oval eyes near its upper area, a simple pair of blue/cyan mountain peaks and one small cyan circular sun in its lower image area. Document should be distinctive and cheerful but restrained; compact, highly legible at 32px. Clean precise silhouettes, rounded corners, shallow depth only, soft internal shadows. No text, no letters, no border around whole canvas, no separate floating decorative marks, no ground plane, no cast shadow beyond the icon silhouette, no photographic texture, no owl, no Duolingo logo. Final production app icon, not a mockup or contact sheet. 1024x1024 square.
