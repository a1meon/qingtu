## Proposed motion after selection

- Primary button: 3–4 px press, short spring on release; preserve bottom-edge depth.
- Dropdown: brief opacity/scale entrance from its trigger, responsive keyboard navigation.
- Import: small entry motion for new queue items; avoid continuous empty-state loops.
- Export success: short bounce on completion, no celebratory overlay covering the image.
- Compare slider: keep direct pointer/rAF updates; no spring or transition on the split line.
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion and keep image processing off the renderer thread.

Preserve current defaults and behavior: empty queue, 10 MB, right inspector, bottom queue, no input/dropdown outer focus halo, actual original/output preview binding, current image engine and target-byte handling. No UI code changed during this exploration.
